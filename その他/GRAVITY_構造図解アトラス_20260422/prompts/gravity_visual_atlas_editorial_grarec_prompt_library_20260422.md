# GRAVITY Visual Atlas Editorial Gralec Prompt Library

## Global style direction

- Create a polished commercial-magazine-grade Japanese explanatory visual.
- Horizontal composition.
- 4K class output feeling, extremely crisp text, maximum legibility.
- White background only.
- Use thin, even monoline drawing.
- Flat 2D vector-like illustration style.
- Very little solid fill.
- Accent colors only in pale pink, mint green, and pale sky blue.
- Tone must be clean, soft, calm, editorial, and professional.
- The style should feel like a refined Japanese web-media / magazine explanatory graphic, not a corporate slide and not a generic AI infographic.
- Use Japanese titles and Japanese callout text.
- The title area should sit at the top with a marker-like pink highlight bar under the main phrase.
- Speech bubbles and annotation boxes should vary subtly in shape, with soft colored outlines.
- The overall goal is not “cute” by itself; it is to create a quiet but strong moment of insight.
- Aim for the simplicity and depth associated with Masahiko Sato style thinking: minimal, clear, intelligent, memorable.
- No meaningless icons.
- No emoji.
- No stock-business pictograms.
- No glossy gradients.
- No dark background.
- No clutter.
- Recompose the information rather than dumping all details equally.
- Make the visual hierarchy obvious at a glance.
