Veil Jourde, counsel to TRAIL in the sale of Buffet Crampon to Alcopa

Veil Jourde is advising TRAIL and its financial co-investors on the sale of Buffet Crampon to Alcopa, a Belgian family investment company.

Established in 1825 and based in Mantes-la-Ville, Buffet Crampon is one of the world’s leading manufacturers and distributors of top-of-the-range wind instruments.

The group is present in a vast segment of its market, serving both professional and amateur musicians, with sales of over €110 million in 2023, 90% of which outside France.

TRAIL’s investment in Buffet Crampon has enabled the group to innovate, consolidate its leadership position through acquisitions and the building of a portfolio of iconic instrument brands, and expand its international presence, particularly in Asia, where the region now accounts for around 25% of its sales.

Buffet Crampon’s strategic transformation in its market illustrates TRAIL’s expertise in developing companies by leveraging their excellence.
