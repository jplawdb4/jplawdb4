Buffet Crampon

Wind instruments manufacturer
Buffet Crampon is a world-renowned manufacturer of wind instruments. It has a comprehensive portfolio of wind instruments (wood and brass) and a developing presence in percussion instruments. The portfolio comprises 12 iconic brands that are recognized by soloists, orchestras, and music schools worldwide for their excellence.
The group has developed its own distribution network across all major geographies as well as showrooms where clients can test, service, and purchase their instruments directly. It designs and manufactures its instruments at six production sites in France, Germany, the USA and China. Through its fully integrated value chain, Buffet Crampon has invested in and developed industry-leading manufacturing capabilities resulting in renowned sound and mechanical quality
