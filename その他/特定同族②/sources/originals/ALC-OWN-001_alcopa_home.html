We are a Belgian family-owned investment company. Founded in 1937, we act as a long-term partner by taking significant positions in sustainable mid-sized European companies.

Alcopa’s ambition is to act as a long-term partner for durable businesses. To achieve it, we articulate our investment strategy around a set of straightforward criteria. To us, the ideal company is...

A strong player in a sector with a positive long term outlook

Making a positive contribution to society

Mature and with a sustainable competitive advantage

Active in manufacturing and/or distributing tangible products

With HQ closely accessible from Belgium

Achieving 10 to 30 M€ EBITDA, or with the ability to reach it in the short term
